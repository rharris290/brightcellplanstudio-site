How to print planner and worksheet PDFs

Page sizes, scaling settings, test pages, paper choice and double-sided tips for clean prints.

SpoonfulOfCalmStudio sells printable PDF planners and journals, and many people also print pages from digital planners or worksheets for a binder or the fridge. This guide covers getting clean, correctly sized prints at home or at a print shop.

1. Check the page size

Each listing explains its page details. Before printing, open the PDF and check the page size in your PDF viewer's document properties or print preview. Common sizes include US Letter (8.5 × 11 in) and A4. They're close, but not identical. If your file and your paper don't match, the print settings below matter.

2. Use a PDF viewer, not a web browser

Open the file in a dedicated PDF reader for the most predictable printing. Browser previews can apply their own scaling, headers or margins.

3. Choose the right scaling setting

In the print dialog, look for scaling options. Names vary by program and printer:

  • Actual size / 100%: prints the page at its true size. Best when your paper matches the PDF's page size.
  • Fit to page / shrink to fit: scales the page to your paper. Best when the sizes differ, or when edges are being cut off.

Most home printers can't print right to the edge of the page, so a small white border is normal.

4. Print a test page first

Print just one page before printing the whole planner:

  • Check that nothing is cut off at the edges
  • Check that lines and text are clear
  • If you'll use a binder or disc system, check that holes won't punch through any content
  • Hold the test page against your binder or folder to check the fit

Most print dialogs let you enter a page range, such as 1 or 5–8, so you don't print everything at once.

5. Pick your paper

  • Everyday printer paper works for checklists and trackers you'll replace often.
  • Heavier paper feels nicer for planners you'll keep, and limits pen bleed-through.
  • Check your printer's manual for the heaviest paper it supports.

6. Printing on both sides

If your printer supports double-sided (duplex) printing, test two pages first to check that the back lines up the right way. For manual double-siding, print odd pages, flip the stack as your printer requires, then print even pages. Do a short test first.

7. Save ink

  • Print only the pages you need this month
  • Use draft or grayscale mode for practice copies
  • Choose pages with less background colour for everyday use

8. Using a print shop

Take the PDF on a USB drive or send it by email, and tell the shop the paper size, paper weight, single or double-sided, and how many copies. Ask for one proof page first.

9. Keep the original file

Save your PDF somewhere safe, so you can reprint pages whenever you need them. Files are for personal use unless the listing says otherwise, so please don't share printed or digital copies.

Problems?

If a page prints oddly after trying these settings, email brightcellplanners@gmail.com with your order details, the page size, and your printer settings, and we'll help.
