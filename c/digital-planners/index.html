About this collection

How to choose

BrightCellPlanStudio has 456 digital planners, trackers and journals, priced from $6.99 to $29.99. They are made for writing by hand on a tablet. Most are hyperlinked, so tabs and links jump straight to the month, section or page you need, and many work in GoodNotes. Each product page lists the apps and devices that planner was designed for.

Pick a starting point below. All-in-one planners keep your whole life in one file. The 201 reMarkable planners are laid out for reMarkable and other e-ink tablets. Business, budget, health, family, wedding and hobby planners each focus on one area, and the bundles group several related planners together.

Dated or undated? Choose dated if you want the calendar ready and plan to start near the beginning of the year. Choose undated if you want to start any month or skip weeks without wasted pages. The guides below explain how to open a hyperlinked planner and how to set one up in GoodNotes.

Want stickers to decorate your pages? The digital stickers collection has pre-cropped PNG sticker sets made for digital planners.

FAQ

Questions and answers

Will a planner work in my app?

Check the product listing, which names the apps or devices the planner was designed for. If your app isn't listed, email before buying.

Should I pick a dated or undated planner?

Pick dated if you want the calendar ready and plan to start near the beginning of that year. Pick undated if you want to start any month or skip weeks without wasted pages.

Which planners are made for reMarkable?

The planners in the reMarkable planners group below are laid out for reMarkable and other e-ink tablets. For a colour tablet, choose one of the other planners.

How quickly will I get my files?

Website Buy Now orders arrive by email within minutes of payment. Etsy orders are delivered by Etsy and appear under Purchases in your Etsy account.

Can I resell or share the files?

Files are for personal use unless the listing says otherwise. You may not resell, share or redistribute them, either as they are or after editing.
