About this collection

How to choose

This is the full collection of 203 spreadsheet templates from BrightCellPlanStudio, priced from $2.99 to $44.99. Each one is a ready-made workbook: you type in your own numbers and the formulas, totals and charts do the rest. The templates are made for Excel and Google Sheets, and the product page for each one says exactly what it supports.

The collection is organised into the groups below. Budget and finance templates (22) cover monthly and annual budgets, the 50/30/20 method, bill calendars, debt payoff and expense tracking. Business dashboards (49) are built for particular kinds of small business and team, while the small business tools (13) handle bookkeeping, client and invoice lists, inventory and pricing. There are also content calendars for creators, health and habit trackers, productivity planners, home and life trackers, event and travel planners, and rental and real estate tools.

Not sure where to start? If you want to get your money in order, begin with a budget template and the beginner guide below. If you run a small business, pick the dashboard or tool closest to the work you do every week, then open the product page to see each tab and column. Bundles group several related spreadsheets together if you want a whole set for one area of life or work.

Spreadsheets suit people who would rather type than handwrite and want the math done for them. If you prefer to write by hand on a tablet, the digital planners collection has matching budget, health and business planners.

They are made for Excel and Google Sheets. Some features can behave a little differently between the two, so the product page is the best guide for each template.

Do I need to know formulas?

No. You type into the input cells and the formulas, totals and charts update on their own. The guides below walk you through a budget spreadsheet step by step.
