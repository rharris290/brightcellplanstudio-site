About this collection

How to choose

This collection has 66 presentation templates, priced from $9.99 to $24.00. There are 49 infographic diagram packs, 16 complete presentation decks and 1 slide bundles. Each product page shows the slides included and the editing apps it supports, and many decks work in Google Slides.

Diagram packs are for dropping a clear visual into a deck you already have: timelines, pricing tables, dashboards and gauges, stat cards, growth trees and more. Presentation decks are complete, ready-to-edit presentations built for a particular kind of business, report or event, so you only swap in your own text, numbers and photos.

The guide below shows how to change colours, fonts and text, add your logo and save a final copy. Try the free Venn diagram slides or the free listing presentation sample first if you want to test the style.
