ADHD planner Excel sample: free one-week focus log

ADHD planner Excel users can try the system before buying: this free sample is one week of the Daily Focus Log from our ADHD Tracker spreadsheet, as a printable PDF you can also fill in on screen.

Get the free sample

Free · PDF, 4 pages, US Letter landscape, fillable · No email or sign-up needed.

What’s in the free sample

  • Start page: what is in the sample and how to use it
  • Week Log: a 7-day Daily Focus Log with columns for focus (1–10), tasks planned and done, inattention, hyperactivity and impulsivity (0–3), sleep, meds, screen time, movement, main distraction, strategy used and notes
  • Week in Pixels: color each day by your focus score, plus boxes for average focus, tasks done, great-focus days and average sleep
  • Example Week: a filled-in week (Jan 7 to Jan 13) so you can see how it works
  • Setup: your details plus the distraction and strategy lists, which you can rename or add to
  • Fillable boxes, so you can type into it in a PDF reader that supports forms, or print it

How to use it

  1. Select Get the free sample to open the PDF.
  2. Print it at 100% / actual size on US Letter, landscape, or open it in a PDF reader that supports forms and type into the boxes.
  3. On the Setup page, cross out or rename distractions and strategies so they fit you.
  4. At the end of each day, log your focus, tasks, symptoms, sleep and more on the Week Log.
  5. Color each Week in Pixels square by your focus score and look back at the week on Sunday.

No. The sample is a printable, fillable PDF of one week. The full ADHD Tracker is a spreadsheet for Excel and Google Sheets with automatic formulas and charts.
