Stoic daily journal for reMarkable: free 2027 sample

Stoic daily journal for reMarkable, with morning prep and evening review pages: this free sample has twelve real pages from the soft color edition (1620 × 2160) of our 2027 Stoic Daily Journal, made for the reMarkable tablet, with working tabs.

Get the free sample

Free · Hyperlinked PDF for reMarkable, 14 portrait pages (1620 × 2160) · No email or sign-up needed.

What’s in the free sample

  • Cover, Home and a How to use this planner page
  • January 2027 calendar (tap a day to open it)
  • Two daily pages, Monday January 4 and Tuesday January 5, each with Morning prep (what matters most, what is in my control, obstacles) and Evening review (what I did well, where I fell short, what I will do better)
  • Virtues section opener with a full-page illustration
  • Virtue tracker for January (wisdom, courage, justice, temperance)
  • Dichotomy of control for January, a monthly review, an undated week page and a lined notes page

How to use it

  1. Select Get the free sample to open the PDF.
  2. Transfer the PDF to your reMarkable with the reMarkable desktop or mobile app (drag and drop), then open it.
  3. Tap the tabs on the top edge to jump between sections; Home returns to the index.
  4. Each morning, fill in Morning prep; each evening, fill in Evening review.
  5. Mark your virtue tracker and use the dichotomy of control page when something is weighing on you.
