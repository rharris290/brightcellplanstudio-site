What’s in the free sample

  • Cover page with a line-art illustration (System / Capture > Move > Finish)
  • Index page with buttons to the Focus Sprint, Kanban and Review samples and the Quick Start guide
  • Focus Sprint Sample 01: boxes for outcome / why it matters, next three actions, and time blocks / distractions to park
  • Kanban Sample 01: Backlog, In progress / limit 3, and Done / evidence
  • Review Sample 01: intended result / actual result, what blocked progress, and keep / change / next experiment
  • A section page before each sample, plus a one-page Quick Start guide
  • Home, Index, Guide, Up, Previous and Next buttons on the edge of every page (61 links in each file)
  • Black-and-white files, plus soft color files for Kindle Scribe Colorsoft, reMarkable Paper Pro and Boox Note Air color models

How to use it

  1. Find your tablet in the table above and pick Portrait or Landscape. Each file has its own fixed layout, and notes don’t move between files.
  2. Send the PDF to your tablet in its original layout, without converting it. Kindle Scribe: use Send to Kindle. reMarkable: drag it into the reMarkable desktop or mobile app, or use the USB web interface. Supernote Manta: copy it into the Document folder with Supernote Partner, Browse & Access or a USB cable. Boox: copy it with a USB cable, BOOXDrop or your cloud drive and open it in NeoReader. Kobo Elipsa 2E: copy it over USB and open it from My Books.
  3. Open the PDF and tap the buttons on the page edge with a fingertip to move around; write with your pen.
  4. Write on a copy and keep the original file as a clean master copy.
