Social media dashboard Google Sheets: free sample

Social media dashboard Google Sheets users can see the whole layout first: this free sample is the Dashboard tab of our Social Media Analytics spreadsheet, filled with example data for a made-up account.

Get the free sample

Free · Spreadsheet (.xlsx) for Google Sheets or Excel · No email or sign-up needed.

What’s in the free sample

  • Free Sample tab: what is included, what the full version adds, and how to open it
  • Dashboard tab with example data for “Fern & Fable Studio”: followers, 30-day reach, engagement rate and 30-day link clicks
  • Followers by platform chart
  • Monthly reach and engagement rate line chart
  • Best posts of the last 90 days by engagements
  • Engagement rate by post type chart
  • Platforms tab (per platform and per post type) and Months tab (12-month trend)

How to use it

  1. Select Get the free sample to get the .xlsx file.
  2. Upload it to Google Drive and open it with Google Sheets (it also opens in Excel).
  3. Start on the Free Sample tab, then open the Dashboard tab.
  4. Explore the charts and the best-posts table.
  5. When you are ready to track your own accounts, get the full version, which builds the dashboard from your posts.
