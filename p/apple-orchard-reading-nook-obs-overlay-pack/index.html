About this product

Description

A light apple orchard reading nook look with soft blob shapes and gentle lines like "Lighting the candles", "Pull up a chair" and "Refilling my tea". It is made for cozy games, reading and chill streams.

Everything is ready to use. Import the OBS scene collection and the scenes are built for you, for regular 16:9 streams and vertical 9:16 streams.

How it works

  1. Buy the pack and get the 4 ZIP files instantly from your Etsy Purchases page
  2. Unzip every ZIP into one folder and import the JSON file in OBS (Scene Collection > Import)
  3. Pick your webcam, game and alerts. The PDF guide walks you through each step.

Please note

  • This is an instant digital file. Nothing physical is shipped.
  • The words on the PNG and video scenes can't be changed. Use the text-free versions or the editable HTML titles to add your own text.
  • PSD source files are not included.
  • Preview images were made with AI help. The demo gameplay, webcam and chat in the photos are for display only.
  • Colors can look slightly different on different screens.
