About this product

Description

Tell your cafe's story with a warm, retro 70s deck full of food photos. Use these 56 slides for investors, landlords, catering clients or staff training: brand story, signature menu, sales numbers, catering packages and growth plans.

Features

  • 56 unique slides, no repeated layouts
  • Light version AND Dark version, each designed in the deck's own palette
  • Photo-rich layouts: full-bleed photos, photo grids, mosaics, collages and shaped photo frames
  • Original photos included and easy to swap: right-click any photo > Change Picture
  • Native, editable PowerPoint charts: right-click > Edit Data and type your numbers
  • Every shape and table is editable: resize, recolor and retype anything
  • Built-in entrance animations
  • 16:9 widescreen, free Google Fonts
  • Placeholder text written for this niche, so you only fill in your details

Compatibility

  • Microsoft PowerPoint 2016 or newer (Windows and Mac)
  • Google Slides (upload the.pptx to Google Drive and open with Google Slides)
  • (open the.pptx on Mac or iPad)
  • Animations play best in PowerPoint. Google Slides shows charts as images after import.

How it works

  1. After purchase, get your ZIP file from your Etsy Purchases page and unzip it.
  2. Install the two free fonts (see the Font guide).
  3. Open the Light or Dark.pptx, replace the placeholder text, numbers and photos, and present.

Please note

  • This is a digital product. No physical item will be shipped.
  • Included photos are original AI-generated images made for this template. Use them inside your own presentations, or swap in your own photos.
  • Sample numbers, prices and names on the slides are placeholders for you to replace.
  • Colors may look slightly different on different screens.
