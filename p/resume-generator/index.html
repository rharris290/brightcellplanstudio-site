About this product

Paste a job post, see your match score. Type your work history once on one Setup sheet. The Resume Generator fills in 4 one-page resume layouts, a cover letter and a thank-you note for you. Paste any job post and it shows a keyword match score (0-100%) for each of your resume versions, with colored bars, the keywords you are missing and the ones you already match. No macros, no scripts, just formulas. Works in desktop spreadsheet apps and in free online sheets (an online sheets copy link is in the How-To guide).

What's inside

  • Setup: up to 4 saved versions, contact details, up to 6 jobs, 36 bullets, 18 skills, 3 schools and 4 certifications. Pick which bullets each version uses and their order with 1, 2, 3.
  • Job Match: paste a job post and get a keyword match score for every version, colored match bars (green, amber, red), missing keywords and the best version for that post.
  • Health Check: red, yellow and green checks for each version, including contact details, summary, one-page fit, dates, weak phrases, numbers in bullets, bullet length, skills, gaps and match.
  • Bullet Builder: turn plain facts into strong bullets, and paste old bullets to find weak phrases like "responsible for" with stronger verbs to use instead.
  • 4 resume layouts: Classic, Modern, Skills First and Highlights. Plain one-column pages with standard headings.
  • Cover letter and thank-you note that fill themselves in from your details and top bullets. Write your own paragraph in any box to replace that part.
  • Career Helpers with ready-to-use wording for job search gaps, caregiving, health or disability breaks, raising children, career changes, first jobs, recent graduates, moving, gig work and returning after a long break.
  • Job Tracker: 60 rows for applications, with version sent, match, status, follow-up dates, interviews, thank-you notes and a next step that updates itself.
  • Bring Your Old Data tab, a How-To guide (PDF), and SAMPLE and BLANK editions, each in Light and Dark.

Who it's for

Anyone who wants to type their work history once and get four resume layouts, a cover letter, a thank-you note and a keyword match score for a job post.

Questions

What is the match score?

The match score counts keywords from the job post that appear in your resume. It is a guide to help you tailor your resume, not a prediction of any hiring decision, and it does not guarantee an interview.

How do I save a resume or letter as a PDF?

Open its tab, then File > Print > Save as PDF (Letter size, one page). The How-To guide walks through it.

Why are the resumes black and white?

The resumes, cover letter and thank-you note are black and white on purpose: plain pages are easy for hiring software to read and clean to print. The Light and Dark editions only change the screen colors of the working tabs.

Does it work in Excel and Google Sheets?

Yes. It uses formulas only, with no macros and no scripts. It works in desktop spreadsheet apps and in free online sheets. An online sheets copy link is in the How-To guide.

How do I get my files?
