About this product

Description

Plan with clarity using this Cash Envelope Budget spreadsheet. Fill in the Setup tab, log as you go, and the Dashboard does the math for you.

What it does

  • Five minutes to set up, a minute after every paycheck
  • Check the groups and ways you pay in step 1. Type the year, how often you are paid and a typical paycheck. Then name up to 30 envelopes: group, cash or digital, a monthly budget and whether leftovers roll over. Sinking funds can have a goal and a date; the planner works out what to put in each month.
  • On payday type the paycheck amount. Every envelope gets its suggested share (monthly budget split across your paychecks). Type a different amount in This paycheck when you want. The bill breakdown tells you exactly how many $100, $50, $20, $10, $5 and $1 bills to ask the bank.
  • Log what goes into each envelope: date, envelope, amount and where it came from. Moving money between envelopes = a minus row in one envelope and a plus row in the other.
  • Log every purchase from an envelope. Left in envelope shows what remains after that purchase and turns red when you overspend.
  • The Dashboard shows every envelope as a card on the envelope wall, with what is left and a bar for how much is used. Pick a month at the top. One Envelope shows a single envelope's full history; Year View compares spending with budget for every month.
  • Yes = leftovers stay in the envelope (best for groceries, sinking funds and savings). No = the envelope starts fresh each month.
  • Not everything has to be cash. Mark an envelope Digital and track it exactly the same way; it is simply left out of the bill breakdown.
