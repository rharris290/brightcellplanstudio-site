HOW TO EDIT

  • Microsoft Word: unzip, open the.docx and click any text to type over it. Change colors with the Home and Shape Format tabs.
  • Google Docs: upload the.docx to Google Drive, then Open with > Google Docs. Edit and save as PDF or Word.
  • Delete or copy sections to fit your experience.

SWAPPING THE PHOTO

  • Word: right-click the placeholder photo > Change Picture > From a File. It keeps the same size and position.
  • Round or arch frames: select your new picture > Picture Format > Crop > Crop to Shape > Oval (or Rounded Rectangle), then Crop > Aspect Ratio 1:1 and drag to frame your face.
  • Google Docs: click the photo > Replace image > Upload from computer. To make it round: Crop (Mask image) > Oval.
  • No photo wanted? Click it and press Delete.
