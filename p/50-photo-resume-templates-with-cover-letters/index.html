Questions and answers

How do I put my own photo in?
In Word, right-click the placeholder photo and choose Change Picture > From a File; it keeps the same size and position. In Google Docs, click the photo and choose Replace image > Upload from computer.

Can I use a design without a photo?
Yes. Click the photo and press Delete, or use designs #045 to #050, which are photo-free minimal styles.

Do I need to buy fonts?
No. All fonts are free Google Fonts, and the README lists them with links. Google Docs already has them under Font > More fonts.

What size are the resumes?
US Letter (8.5 x 11 in), one page per resume.

How do I get the files?

How to edit

  • Microsoft Word: unzip, open the .docx and click any text to type over it. Change colors with the Home and Shape Format tabs.
  • Google Docs: upload the .docx to Google Drive, then Open with > Google Docs. Edit and save as PDF or Word.
  • Delete or copy sections to fit your experience.

Swapping the photo

  • Word: right-click the placeholder photo > Change Picture > From a File. It keeps the same size and position.
  • Round or arch frames: select your new picture > Picture Format > Crop > Crop to Shape > Oval (or Rounded Rectangle), then Crop > Aspect Ratio 1:1 and drag to frame your face.
  • Google Docs: click the photo > Replace image > Upload from computer. To make it round: Crop (Mask image) > Oval.
  • No photo wanted? Click it and press Delete.
  • Designs #045-#050 in this bundle are photo-free minimal / ATS-friendly styles.
