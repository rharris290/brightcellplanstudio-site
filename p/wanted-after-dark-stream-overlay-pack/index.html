About this product

Description

🚨 WANTED AFTER DARK STREAM PACK: gritty rainy nights and police lights for your stream 🌧️

Rain-slick city streets glowing with red and blue police lights, a moonlit swamp at night with cypress trees, fireflies and glowing gator eyes, and a rooftop skyline with a helicopter spotlight sweeping the city. Stencil-style titles with a red-to-blue siren glow and a lightbar finish the look. It's made for open-world crime, heist, getaway and driving game streams, roleplay servers and late-night sessions.

Everything is ready to use. An OBS import file builds all 10 scenes in minutes, and your webcam and chat are separate pieces you can move and resize however you like.

How it works

  1. Buy the pack and get the ZIP right away from your Etsy Purchases page
  2. Unzip it and import the JSON file in OBS (Scene Collection > Import)
  3. Pick your webcam, game and chat link, then drag your cam and chat wherever you like. The guide walks you through each step, including how to re-link files if OBS asks.

Please note

  • This is a digital file. Nothing physical is shipped.
  • You can't edit the words on the text versions. Use the textless versions to add your own text in OBS.
  • Animated alerts, a stinger transition and PSD source files are NOT included.
  • The demo gameplay, webcam and chat in the photos are for display only.
  • Colors can look slightly different on different screens.
