Description

🎮 OPEN WORLD STREAM OVERLAY MEGA BUNDLE: 6 complete stream packs, 60 OBS scenes

Switch up your stream's look any time you like. This bundle gives you all six of my open-world themed overlay packs: a neon synthwave city, a sunny beach coast, a rainy night city with police lights, a luxury sunset marina, a misty swamp and a late-night street race. Each pack is complete, with its own OBS import file that builds 10 ready-made scenes, plus movable cam and chat, animated loops and Twitch panels.

💰 BUNDLE PRICE: $34.99 for all 6 packs. That's less than buying 4 packs separately at $9.99 each ($39.96), and you save $24.95 compared with buying all 6 one at a time ($59.94).

How it works

  1. Buy the bundle and get all 5 files right away from your Etsy Purchases page. Etsy allows 5 files per listing, so 4 files hold one pack each and the 5th file holds Marina Glow and Coastline Heat together. You get all 6 complete packs.
  2. Unzip the pack you want and import its JSON file in OBS (Scene Collection > Import). Each pack becomes its own scene collection, so you can switch looks from the Scene Collection menu.
  3. Pick your webcam, game and chat link, then drag your cam and chat wherever you like. Each pack's guide walks you through every step, including how to re-link files if OBS asks.
