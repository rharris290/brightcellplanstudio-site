About this product

Description

Budgeting on a fixed or tight income is its own kind of hard. This calm, minimalist Budget & Bill Tracker is made for real life on disability benefits, pensions, part-time work, or any income that doesn't stretch far. It's clear and uncluttered, with no judgment.

Every amount has a job, no bill slips through, and small wins are easy to see.

How it works

  1. Purchase and get the ZIP from your Etsy account (Purchases & Reviews), then unzip it.
  2. To print: open a Print PDF and print at home or at a print shop at 100% / actual size. Print as many copies as you need for your own use.
  3. To fill in digitally: open a Fillable PDF in the free Adobe Acrobat Reader on your computer, tablet or phone. Type into the fields, tick the boxes, and save.

Good to know

  • instant digital file. No physical item will be shipped.

This planner is an organizing tool, not financial, legal or benefits advice.

What you get

What’s included

WHAT'S INCLUDED (12 pages)

  • Cover with contents
  • Annual Overview: income, bills, spending, saved, debt paid and left over for all 12 months, plus yearly goals and important dates (benefit reviews, renewals, taxes)
  • Monthly Budget: income vs. expected, fixed bills, everyday spending, savings and debt (budget vs. actual), plus an end-of-month check-in
  • Bill Payment Tracker: each bill once with due day, amount, autopay, and a tick box for every month of the year
  • Expense Log: date, what/where, category, paid with, need or want, amount and monthly total
  • Subscription Tracker: cost, monthly/yearly, renewal, free-trial end date, keep/pause/cancel decision, plus monthly and yearly totals
  • Benefits & Income Tracker: 4 income-source cards (amount, pay day, case/ref #, next review) and a payment log with expected vs. received
  • Savings Goals: 4 goals with progress bars and deposit logs
  • Debt Payoff Tracker: debt list, snowball / avalanche / hardship-plan strategy, debt-free progress bar and payment log
  • Sinking Funds: 6 funds for known costs (medical, equipment, gifts, vet...) with monthly tick boxes
  • No-Spend Challenge: 30-day tracker, what's still okay to spend on, money saved, reflections
  • Money Contacts & Notes: who to call about bills and benefits, help programs to look into, and call notes
