About this product

Description

Managing a chronic illness can feel like a part-time job: medications, refills, appointments, test results, and the energy it all takes. This calm Spoonie Health Planner Bundle brings three gentle printables together in one purchase, so your meds, your appointments and your spoons all live in one place.

Made for spoonies, people managing chronic illness, fatigue or chronic pain, people juggling several specialists, and caregivers looking after a loved one.

THIS BUNDLE INCLUDES 3 PRINTABLES (20 pages in total)

1. MEDICATION TRACKER & SCHEDULE (7 pages)

Keep every dose, refill and side effect in one place, so you can stop second-guessing ("did I already take that?") and walk into appointments and the pharmacy prepared.

  • Cover page with name and start date
  • My Medication List: medication or supplement, dose, when / how often, purpose, prescriber, pharmacy and refill date, plus a section for stopped or past medications and why they were stopped
  • Daily Medication Checklist: 31 days with morning, midday, evening and bedtime check-offs, a space for the time taken, and notes for missed or late doses
  • Weekly Pill Schedule: a 7-day x 4-time grid laid out like a pill organizer, with dose times, "with food?" boxes, special instructions and a pill organizer routine
  • Refill Tracker: two pharmacy contact cards plus Rx number, quantity, refills left, last filled, next due, ordered and picked-up check boxes, and space for prior authorization and insurance notes
  • Side Effects & Notes Log: date, medication, what you noticed, a 1-5 severity scale, how long it lasted, a "told my doctor" check box, plus questions for your doctor or pharmacist
  • As-Needed (PRN) Dose Log: cards for up to 3 as-needed medications (dose, used for, max in 24 hours, time between doses) and a log of date, time, dose, reason, relief (1-5) and when the next dose is OK

2. DOCTOR APPOINTMENT PREP & VISIT LOG (6 pages)

Plan what to say before you go, write down what was said while you're there, and keep results, contacts and follow-ups together.

  • Cover page with name and start date
  • Appointment Prep Sheet: appointment details, an "if I only get to say one thing today" line, your top 3 concerns (and how long they've been going on), a bring-with-me checklist, symptoms and changes since your last visit with a 1-5 "impact on daily life" scale, questions to ask (tick when answered), and medications to review
  • Visit Notes: visit details and type (in person, telehealth, phone), what the doctor said, tests ordered, referrals, medication changes (new, changed, stopped), next steps and follow-up, a "before I leave the room" checklist, and "did I feel heard?"
  • Test & Lab Results Tracker: date, test, ordered by, result, normal range, flag (copied from your lab report) and follow-up notes, plus a "results I watch over time" grid to compare up to 6 checks side by side
  • Care Team & Specialist Contacts: 6 contact cards (primary care, specialists, therapist or other) with clinic, phone, fax, portal or email and address, plus after-hours, urgent care, hospital and insurance numbers
  • Appointment Calendar & Log: an undated month view (fits any month and year) plus a log of date, time, doctor, purpose, prepped, attended and follow-up

3. SPOON THEORY ENERGY PACING PLANNER (7 pages)

Budget the energy you have, learn what activities really cost you, notice boom-and-bust patterns, and make rest part of the plan, without guilt.

  • Cover page with name and start date
  • Daily Spoon Budget: tick the spoons you woke up with, set a reserve, then use a spoon ledger (time, activity, cost, boost, running balance, done) to plan your day, with an end-of-day balance ("borrowed from tomorrow?") and a "learning my budget" section
  • Activity Spoon-Cost Reference: rate everyday activities from 1-5 spoons across self-care, home, out & about, people, and work/study/admin, plus your own activities, with space for an easier version of each
  • Weekly Pacing Plan: your baseline, rest days, and a 7-day grid for expected spoons, one big thing, smaller tasks, rest blocks (AM/PM/evening), spoons used and how it went, plus a must-do planner and an "if a flare hits, I'll let go of" list
  • Boom & Bust Tracker: a monthly energy graph (one dot per day, 0-10), daily activity and symptom scores, flare/crash check boxes, a boom & bust episode log, early warning signs and what helps you recover
  • Rest & Recovery Log: five types of rest (physical, mental, sensory, emotional, other), energy before and after (1-5), planned rest, what helped, and a recovery plan for big days
  • Low Spoon Day Menu: essentials-only and "a little more" task lists, shortcuts & swaps, easy foods, who can help with what, and a permission slip for hard days

How it works

  1. Purchase and get the ZIP file from your Etsy account (Purchases & Reviews), then unzip it.
  2. To print: open a Print PDF and print at home or at a print shop at 100% / actual size. Print as many copies as you need for your own use, e.g. one prep sheet and visit notes page per appointment.
  3. To fill in digitally: open a Fillable PDF in the free Adobe Acrobat Reader on your computer or tablet. Type into the fields, tap the check boxes, spoons and dots, and save.
