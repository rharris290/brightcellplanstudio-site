About this product

Description

Caring for someone you love is a lot to hold. This calm, organized Caregiver & Medical Binder puts medications, appointments, contacts, daily care, and important records in one place, so you, your family, and every helper are on the same page.

You get THREE binder sets in one purchase. Start simple, or use the whole thing:

  • STARTER SET (5 pages) ~

Quick essentials for a short-term need or a lighter care load:

  • Cover
  • Patient Profile & Emergency Info
  • Medication List
  • Daily Care Log
  • Important Contacts
  • ESSENTIALS SET (15 pages) ~

Everything in Starter, plus:

  • Doctor & Care Team Directory
  • Insurance & Benefits
  • Medication Administration Record (MAR), a monthly 31-day grid
  • Pharmacy & Refill Tracker
  • Appointment Log
  • Questions for the Doctor
  • Vitals Log (blood pressure, pulse, temperature, glucose, weight, O2)
  • Symptom Log
  • Hospital Stay Log (admission, daily updates, discharge checklist)
  • Notes
  • COMPLETE SET (35 pages, 5 tabbed sections) ~

Everything in Essentials, plus:

  • Contents page + 5 section dividers with tabs
  • Medical History (conditions, surgeries, family history, vaccinations)
  • Allergy & Reaction Record
  • Emergency Go-Bag Checklist
  • Wound & Skin Care Log
  • Monthly Overview Calendar
  • Weekly Care Planner
  • Shift Handoff Notes
  • Meal & Hydration Log
  • Personal Care & Mobility Checklist (bathing, hygiene, movement)
  • Equipment & Supplies Inventory
  • Medical Expense Tracker
  • Legal & Important Documents Checklist (advance directive, POA, POLST and more: a checklist of what exists and where it's kept)
  • Accounts & Services List (account names and phone numbers only, with NO password fields)
  • Caregiver Self-Care page

What you receive (one zip file)

  • Starter: print PDF + fillable PDF
  • Essentials: print PDF + fillable PDF
  • Complete: print PDF + fillable PDF + editable DOCX (Word / Google Docs, simpler styling)
  • How_To_Use.txt with printing and filling tips

Formats & size

  • US Letter (8.5 x 11 in). A4 users: print with "Fit to page".
  • Print PDF: print at home or at a copy shop, as many times as you need for your household.
  • Fillable PDF: type into the lines and tick the checkboxes in Adobe Acrobat Reader (free), Apple Preview, or a web browser. Save and print, or share a copy with family.
  • Editable DOCX (Complete set): change headings, add rows, and rename sections in Microsoft Word, Google Docs, Pages, or LibreOffice.
  • Calm, muted colors with white page interiors, so it's easy on your printer ink.

  • This is a digital file. Nothing is shipped.
  • Colors may look a little different depending on your screen and printer.

  • This binder is an organizing and tracking tool. It is not medical, legal, or financial advice. Always follow the guidance of the care team and qualified professionals.
