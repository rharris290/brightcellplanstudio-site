Built for e-ink

  • Made for reMarkable Paper Pro (11.8 inch colour screen, 1620x2160), at its exact screen size. Soft colour art tuned for colour e-ink; it also reads clearly in black and white.
  • Sleep screens are portrait: this tablet shows its sleep screen upright, even when you write sideways. No landscape sleep screens are included for that reason.
  • Every page is at most 2 taps away, with the index or tabs on every page. Links work both ways: tap in, tap back.
  • Wide toolbar-safe margins in both orientations, and large tap targets (at least 9 mm).
  • Soft, low-saturation colour line art with dark outlines, so it stays clear on colour e-ink and in black and white.

PLEASE READ BEFORE BUYING: this tablet has no built-in option for custom sleep screens. Changing the system sleep image needs developer mode and third-party tools, and software updates can undo it. The guide explains this honestly; every screen also works as page art without any system changes.

How to use

  • Send the PDF to your e-ink tablet with its companion app, a cloud folder, email-to-device or USB, then open it and tap the tabs with a fingertip.
  • Write anywhere: it also works in PDF and note-taking apps on other tablets, phones and computers, and the pages can be printed.
