Who it's for

Placeholder content is written for recent graduates and early-career seekers: a short profile, key skills, internships or part-time roles, projects, education, and certifications.

How it works

  1. Purchase and get the .zip file from your Etsy account.
  2. Unzip it and open the .docx in Microsoft Word, or upload it to Google Drive and choose Open with > Google Docs.
  3. Click any placeholder text and type your own. Delete sections you don't need.
  4. Save or get as a PDF and send it to employers.

Good to know

  • Built with real Word styles, so formatting stays consistent as you edit.
  • You won't need to buy any fonts. Some designs use Word fonts (Arial, Georgia, Calibri, Cambria); others use free Google Fonts that work automatically in Google Docs and can be installed free for Word. The buyer guide lists the fonts.
  • Colors and fonts can be changed in Word or Google Docs.
  • This is a digital file. No physical item will be shipped.
  • Colors may look slightly different on different screens.
