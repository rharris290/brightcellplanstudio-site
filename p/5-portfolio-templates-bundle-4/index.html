HOW TO EDIT

  • Microsoft Word: unzip, open the.docx and click any text to type over it. Change colors with the Home and Shape Format tabs.
  • Google Docs: upload the.docx to Google Drive, then Open with > Google Docs. Edit and save as PDF or Word.
  • Delete or copy sections to fit your experience.

SWAPPING THE IMAGES

  • Word: right-click a placeholder image > Change Picture > From a File. It keeps the same size and position.
  • Round, arch or rounded frames: Picture Format > Crop > Crop to Shape (Oval, Rounded Rectangle) and Crop > Aspect Ratio to match.
  • Google Docs: click the image > Replace image > Upload from computer; use Crop (Mask image) for round shapes.
  • For sharp prints use images at least 2200 px wide for full-width spots.

FONTS
