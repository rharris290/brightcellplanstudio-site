About this product

Overview

Your year in money, wrapped up on its own. Log your money moves through the year and Money Wrapped builds a bold, shareable recap for you: ten story-size cards (9:16) with one big stat each, plus a full dashboard. No macros, no scripts, just formulas. Works in desktop spreadsheet apps and in free online sheets.

What’s inside

  • Ten story-size recap cards (9:16), one big stat each, from your intro and savings rate to your top categories, biggest purchase, best month and no-spend streak
  • Setup: your name, any year 1900-2200, your currency symbol, 24 editable categories with vibe tags
  • Transactions log: 2,500 rows with dropdown categories and checks on dates and amounts
  • Dashboard: money in, money out, kept, savings rate, no-spend streak, personality, month table, in vs out chart, top 5 categories, favorite places, highlights
  • Bring Your Old Data tab: move your entries into a newer version in about 3 minutes
  • How-To guide (PDF)
  • SAMPLE and BLANK editions, each in Light and Dark

Who it’s for

Anyone who logs their spending through the year and wants an automatic, shareable year-in-review of where the money went, what they kept and their money personality.

Questions and answers

What is on the cards?
Ten story-size cards with one big stat each: your year intro, what you kept and your savings rate, your number 1 and top 5 categories, your favorite spot, your biggest purchase, your best and biggest spend months, a month by month chart, your longest no-spend streak and your money personality.

Do bills show up in the top lists?
Bills and essentials stay out of the top lists by default so the cards show the fun stuff; one switch turns them on.

Can I use my own currency and categories?
Yes. Setup takes your name, any year from 1900 to 2200, your currency symbol and 24 editable categories.

How do I get my files?
This is a digital product, so nothing is shipped. If you buy on Etsy, the files are on your Etsy Purchases page right after checkout.
