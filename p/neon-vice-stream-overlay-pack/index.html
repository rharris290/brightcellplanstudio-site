🌴 NEON VICE STREAM PACK: retro Miami nights for your stream 🌅

Take your stream to a neon-soaked coastal city at sunset: pink and orange skies, a glowing striped sun, palm tree silhouettes, a retro grid and a city skyline full of lit windows. It's made for open-world crime and driving game streams, 80s synthwave fans, chill Just Chatting sessions and anyone who loves that retro Miami neon look.

Everything is ready to use. An OBS import file builds all 10 scenes in minutes, and your webcam and chat are separate pieces you can move and resize however you like.

🧩 EVERY BOX IS MOVABLE

  • Every box is its own layer inside its own unlocked OBS group, in every scene: Cam Box, Chat, Follower / Donation / Socials labels, Alerts Box, Replay frame and the Title Panel on each screen
  • Click a group, drag it anywhere, resize it from the corners, hide it or delete it. Nothing is baked into one big overlay
  • Backgrounds come box-free, so the title panels move freely too (the original all-in-one screens are included, hidden, if you prefer them)
  • Extra cam styles ready to switch on: alternate colour and circle

📷 CAMERA PRE-FITTED

  • A Webcam source already sits inside every cam frame, sized and masked to the opening, so no corners poke out
  • Just double-click Webcam and pick your camera once, and it works in every scene
  • Illustrated "Fit your camera" page in the setup guide, with real OBS screenshots: picking the device, moving and resizing, cropping, re-fitting with Edit Transform, mirroring, and fixing a black camera

⭐ WHAT'S INCLUDED

  • OBS Studio scene collection (Scene Collection > Import) with 10 scenes already built: Starting Soon, Live Gameplay, Gameplay Fullscreen (cam only), Just Chatting, Be Right Back, Intermission, Technical Difficulties, Replay / Highlights, Ending and Offline
  • EVERY BOX IS MOVABLE: cam, chat, labels, alerts, replay frame and title panels are separate layers in unlocked OBS groups (details below)
  • 7 full screens (1920x1080 PNG): Starting Soon, Be Right Back, Intermission, Technical Difficulties, Just Chatting, Stream Ending, Offline
  • 4 TEXTLESS backgrounds, so you can add your own words
  • 2 ANIMATED 10-second loops (MP4): Starting Soon with neon flicker and shimmering water, and Be Right Back with a moving retro grid
  • Overlay pieces: small and large webcam frames, small and large chat frames, an Instant Replay frame, and Follower, Donation, Subscriber, Socials and blank label bars
  • 4 extra webcam frames: 16:9 alternate color, 4:3, circle and vertical 9:16
  • Alert box background (transparent PNG)
  • 7 Twitch panels (320x160): About Me, Schedule, Rules, Donate, Socials, Discord and Blank
  • Friendly step-by-step setup guide (PDF + TXT), including how to move and resize your cam and chat, plus a list of free fonts for commercial use

⭐ WORKS WITH OBS Studio (one-click import) · Streamlabs Desktop · StreamElements (add the PNG/MP4 files by hand) Stream on Twitch, YouTube, Kick, TikTok Live and Facebook Gaming

⭐ HOW IT WORKS

  1. Buy the pack and get the ZIP right away from your Etsy Purchases page
  2. Unzip it and import the JSON file in OBS (Scene Collection > Import)
  3. Pick your webcam, game and chat link, then drag your cam and chat wherever you like. The guide walks you through each step, including how to re-link files if OBS asks.

⭐ PLEASE NOTE

  • This is a DIGITAL PRODUCT. Nothing physical is shipped.
  • You can't edit the words on the text versions. Use the textless versions to add your own text in OBS.
  • Animated alerts, a stinger transition and PSD source files are NOT included.
  • The demo gameplay, webcam and chat in the photos are for display only.
  • Colors can look slightly different on different screens.
