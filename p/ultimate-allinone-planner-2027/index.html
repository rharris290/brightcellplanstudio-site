About this product

Overview

This 2027 all-in-one GoodNotes digital planner puts a complete dated 2027 calendar and 112 ready-to-use templates into one 571-page hyperlinked PDF. The templates sit in 12 color-coded sections, from Personal and Budget to Work, Travel and Content Creator, and each section opens with its own painted illustration.

What’s inside

  • Calendar: Year at a glance, 12 monthly calendars with plan and review pages, 53 weekly spreads and 365 dated daily pages

  • 12 sections: Personal (16), Self-Care (8), Education (8), Finance (8), Budget (8), Vision (8), Planning (11), Household (9), Work (9), Travel (8), Health and Wellness (10), Content Creator (9)

Who it’s for

Anyone who wants one GoodNotes planner for the whole year instead of several separate ones, and who prefers writing by hand. It is a handwriting planner, so calculations are not automatic.

Compatible apps

Write on it anywhere

  • Every page is writable: open the PDF in GoodNotes or another PDF note app and write with an, any stylus or your finger.
  • Works on iPad, Android tablets, iPhone and Android phones. You can also type text boxes and add stickers.
  • Tabs and links keep working while you write (in GoodNotes, switch to read-only mode to tap links, then back to write).
  • On a phone the screen is small, so zooming in or using a stylus makes handwriting easiest.
  • A note-taking app is needed to write on the pages. A basic PDF viewer will only show them.

How to use

  1. Open your Etsy purchase and save the files (unzip the ZIPs).
  2. Import the PDF for your theme or edition into your note-taking app.
  3. Tap the tabs, calendar days and tiles to move around; write with your stylus.
  4. Use the sticker book with the lasso tool, or import the PNG stickers into your app's stickers or elements library.
