About this product

Description

Track what you earn and spend, see what to set aside for your 2027 quarterly tax payments, and get a year-end summary ready for tax time.

14 tabs that work together

  • Home: opens first, with live tiles, a month picker, alert banners, plain-English insights and buttons to every tab
  • Smart Setup: currency, starting month and colour theme
  • Setup: your tax rates (Social Security, Medicare, standard deduction, income and state rates), last year's tax for safe harbor, clients and expense categories
  • Income: one row per payment; tax quarter and month fill in on their own
  • Expenses: categories mapped to Schedule C lines, meals at 50% and a business-use % column
  • Invoices: payments are matched by invoice number, with balance, status (Paid, Part paid, Open, Overdue) and days late
  • Quarterly Tax: estimates self-employment, income and state tax from your profit so far, with a suggested payment for each due date (April 15, June 15, September 15, 2027 and January 15, 2028), days left, status and the safe-harbor amount
  • Dashboard: income, expenses, profit, tax for the year, next payment, unpaid invoices and charts
  • Year-End: totals by Schedule C line, a tax summary and the clients who may send you a 1099-NEC
  • What-If: try different values and see now vs what-if side by side without changing your data
  • Heatmap: every day of the year as a coloured square with monthly totals
  • Search: type a name, word or date and see matching rows
  • Year in Review: the last 12 months on one printable page
  • How to Use

Works in Microsoft Excel 2016 or newer (Windows and Mac) and Google Sheets. No macros. Upload the xlsx to Google Drive and open it with Google Sheets; the charts keep working.

Good to know

  • Type only in the shaded cells. Formula cells are locked without a password so they don't get erased by accident.
  • Smart Setup: pick your currency, the month your year starts and one of 15 themes (Original plus 14 colour themes).
  • This is a digital file. Nothing physical will be shipped.
  • Tax numbers are estimates for planning. Rates and limits change, so check them each year and ask a tax professional about your own situation.
